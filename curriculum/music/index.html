Our approach

Intent
Our aim is to provide children with opportunities to perform, compose, listen and appraise across a range of music, through a broad and balanced curriculum.
Implementation
Music teaching delivers the requirements of the National Curriculum through our own subject map and the Kapow Primary scheme. Classroom teachers deliver weekly lessons.
Impact
We measure impact through pupil voice questionnaires, assessment recorded on Sonar, and photo and video evidence of the children performing.
